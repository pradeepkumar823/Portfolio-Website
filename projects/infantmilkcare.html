Project Overview

InfantMilkCare is a precision health monitoring web platform designed to help parents track their infant's milk intake, growth statistics, and feeding alarms. Built as a fully secure Spring Boot MVC application, it acts as a smart companion to record and visualize infant nutrition.

The system features multi-child profiles, allowing parents to switch seamlessly between children via a session-based active dropdown. It also implements automated growth status badges (calculating whether an infant is underweight, healthy, or overweight based on age), and features a barcode scanner simulator that matches BIS compliance and triggers allergen warnings.

Key Features

Smart Dashboard

Real-time overview of the child's weight, hemoglobin levels, age, and color-coded growth badges.

Feeding Log

Track and log every feed with type (Formula, Breast Milk, Water), amount in mL, time, and custom notes.

Feeding Alarms

Schedule alarms and custom reminders to ensure infants are fed on time, manageable directly from the dashboard.

Formula Scanner

Simulate barcode scans to identify ingredients, allergen alerts, and verify BIS compliance on formulas.

Privacy Shield

Secures children's private data, automatically masking full names with initials across the UI headers.

Multi-Child Profiles

Easily manage logs and records for multiple children under a single account with dynamic session switching.
